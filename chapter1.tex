\section{Background}

According to \textcite{kioko-etal-2003}, the Harvard method is essential 
for academic writing. This is further supported by research showing that 
\parencite[45]{ellis-peters-2000} proper citation practices improve 
academic integrity.

\textcite{henderson-etal-1987} conducted a comprehensive study demonstrating 
the importance of consistent referencing. Their findings \parencite[67]{henderson-etal-1987} 
indicate that most citation errors occur when authors fail to include page 
numbers in their citations.

\section{Literature Review}

Recent work in the field \parencite{oliver-2006,delacalle-rodriguez-2021} has shown that 
multiple methodological approaches exist. However, \textcite{dalicuba-2020} 
argues that a unified approach is preferable.

Online resources also provide valuable guidance \parencite{kabaso-krishanth}. 
Interviews with experts \parencite{hendricks-waldon} reveal that practice is 
essential for mastering this style.

\section{Conclusion}

The Harvard method, as described by multiple authors \parencite{kioko-etal-2003,
	ellis-peters-2000,henderson-etal-1987}, remains the standard for academic 
writing in South Africa.
